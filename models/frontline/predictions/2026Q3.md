## The constraint prediction 4 violates

Across 17 quarters, Frontline's realised VLCC spot TCE has **never** exceeded
the covered rate it reported for that quarter:

- mean 0.844, median 0.841, sd 0.055
- range 0.725 (2022Q2) to 0.966 (2026Q1)
- exceedances: 0 of 17

The kernel predicts 165,346 / 156,900 = **1.054**, outside the entire observed
range in a direction never once recorded.

## What each outcome would mean

- **near 140k** — the tail-weighting defect is the answer: late feed weeks enter
  the kernel at full value when the quarter's remaining days are mostly ballast.
  `a` stays at 14.
- **near 155k** — the lag is doing more work than the back-test suggested, and
  `a` should rise toward the measured ballast leg (27.5d from fixtures).
- **above 157k** — the 17-quarter constraint broke, and the reasoning in this
  file is wrong. Record that plainly.

## What it settled

**The constraint held.** Realised / covered = 148,500 / 156,900 = **0.947**,
inside the 0.725–0.966 range. Still never above 1.0 in 18 quarters. Prediction
4 required 1.054 and was therefore outside the whole observed range before the
quarter began — the clearest signal available, and it was right.

**The outcome fell between the two scenarios this file set out.** Neither
"near 140k — the tail-weighting defect" nor "near 155k — the lag is doing more
work" is the answer at 148,500. The scenarios were too far apart to
discriminate, which is a defect in how this file was written.

**The uplift did NOT degrade at high rates.** 2026Q3 capture was 0.943 against
a mean of 1.012. That was the feared failure, but it is not what happened:
corr(blend level, ratio) = −0.30 at n=9, and the two lowest ratios in the
sample are 0.937 at a blend of 36,618 and 0.943 at 157,473 — opposite ends of
the rate range. 0.943 is −1.4 sd from the mean. Ordinary dispersion.

**The EPS bridge was right.** TCE-to-profit gap calibrated on 2026Q2 at 173.1
came in at 172.932, off 0.1%. Model Q3 EPS 2.42 against 2.38 reported, off
1.7%. The charter rate in charters.csv, 89,771, against Frontline's disclosed
VLCC time charter TCE of 90,800, off 1.1%. Everything downstream of the rate
forecast held. The failure was isolated to the rate.

**Dividend = adjusted EPS to the cent** for a fifth straight quarter: $2.38.

## What changed as a result

- uplift VLCC 1.02 → **1.012**, Suezmax 1.03 → **1.026** and now MEASURED
- k re-measured over 18 quarters: VLCC 0.26 → **0.28**, Suezmax 0.55 → **0.54**,
  LR2 0.45 → **0.46**. Medians barely moved; the spread (sd 0.175 on VLCC) is
  the finding, and it is now recorded in config.
- 2026Q3 added to the back-test at its reported value. Mean absolute error
  rises 2.9% → 4.0%, which is the first honest out-of-sample number this model
  has had.
